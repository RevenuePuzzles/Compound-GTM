# Plays — what we run, and what it has taught us

> Fictional example.

## New-client-win outbound to agency ops

- **Status:** working
- **For:** Directors of client services or operations · holding-company agencies, 150–800 people
- **Trigger:** the agency announced a new client win in the last 60 days
- **Shape:** signal-led outbound, 3 emails + 1 LinkedIn touch over 12 days, sent by the founder
- **Steps:** see `motions/2026-09-outbound-agency-ops/assets/sequence.md`
- **Success criterion:** ≥ 8 meetings per 250 contacts within 8 weeks
- **Kill criterion:** < 3 positive replies after the first 100 contacts
- **Runs:**
  - 2026-09 · `motions/2026-09-outbound-agency-ops` — 11 meetings / 240 contacts — hit
  - 2026-10 · `motions/2026-10-uk-agency-ops` — planned
- **Rules learned:**
  - Name the client win in the first line; never guess at internal details. (Observed, directional · 13/120 vs 4/120 · 2026-09)
  - Lowercase, internal-style subject lines outperformed title-case ones. (Observed, directional · 19/118 vs 12/118 replies · 2026-09 — under 30 replies per variant)
  - Reply within one business day to "not now"; three of the eleven meetings came from the second reply. (Observed · 3 of 11 meetings · 2026-09)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-03
