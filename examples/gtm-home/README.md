# Example GTM home: Quillmark

> **Fictional.** Quillmark, its customers, the people quoted and every number in this folder are invented to show how a GTM home looks after a few motions. Nothing here describes a real company.

**Quillmark** sells a client-reporting and measurement platform to agencies owned by advertising holding companies.

## How to read it

Start with the story, in order:

1. [`motions/2026-07-outbound-holdco-data-leads/`](motions/2026-07-outbound-holdco-data-leads/) — the first outbound campaign. It **missed**. The retro turned nine replies into four lessons.
2. [`motions/2026-09-outbound-agency-ops/`](motions/2026-09-outbound-agency-ops/) — the next campaign, built from those lessons. It **hit** its target, and the play was promoted to `working`.
3. [`motions/2026-10-uk-agency-ops/`](motions/2026-10-uk-agency-ops/) — the third, planned straight from the play library to see whether it holds in a second market (the bar for `proven`).

Then open the home files and look for the dated, evidence-tagged lines those retros wrote:

- `ICP.md` — the buyer moved from holding-company data leads to agency operations leads.
- `POSITIONING.md` — the message moved from "media efficiency" to "client-reporting hours".
- `PLAYS.md` — one play retired, one working.
- `PROOF.md` — note the claim tagged `Believed · internal` that the team is not allowed to use yet.
