# ICP — who buys, who doesn't, and why now

> Fictional example. Every line carries an evidence level and a source.

## Company fit

- Agencies owned by an advertising holding company, 150–800 people, running reporting for 20+ client brands. (Observed ×6 · qualified opps from the Sept outbound · 2026-09)
- Reporting assembled by account and client-services teams in spreadsheets and slides, not by a central data team. (Heard ×6 · discovery calls · 2026-09)
- ~~Holding-company central data and analytics units.~~ Moved to disqualifiers 2026-08-04 — see below.

## Situation — when they need us

- Onboarding a newly won client: reporting has to be rebuilt from scratch within the first 4–8 weeks. (Heard ×5 · discovery calls · 2026-09)
- Client-services team losing senior people to reporting work instead of client strategy. (Heard ×3 · discovery calls · 2026-09)

## Triggers — what opens the window

| Trigger | Why it opens a window | How we detect it | Window | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agency announces a new client win | New reporting setup, deadline pressure, budget attached to the account | Trade press, agency news pages, LinkedIn announcements | ~60 days | Observed (directional) — 13 positive replies / 120 trigger-based contacts vs 4 / 120 static list · 2026-09 |
| New head of client services or operations | New leaders review tooling in the first 90 days | Job changes on LinkedIn | ~90 days | Believed — untested |

## Buying committee

| Role | What they care about (their words) | Influence | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Director of client services / agency operations | "My best people spend Mondays building decks" | Champion, often signs entry offer | Heard ×6 · 2026-09 |
| Agency managing director | Margin on the account; keeping the client past year one | Signs core contract | Heard ×2 · 2026-09 |
| Holding-company data / tech governance | Data security, approved-vendor list, no duplicate tools | Can block | Heard ×3 · discovery calls · 2026-09 · internal |

## Pains in their words

- "Every new client means rebuilding the reporting from zero." (Heard ×5 · discovery calls · 2026-09)
- "Reporting is eating my account team." (Heard ×4 · discovery calls · 2026-09)

## Disqualifiers — when we walk away

- Holding-company central data and analytics units as the entry point.
  - They don't own client reporting — it sits with the agencies. (Heard ×4 · replies · 2026-07)
  - They already run marketing-mix and attribution vendors. (Heard ×2 · replies · 2026-07)
- Agencies under 100 people: one person does reporting part-time; no budget line. (Observed · 0 qualified from 3 small-agency meetings · 2026-09)
