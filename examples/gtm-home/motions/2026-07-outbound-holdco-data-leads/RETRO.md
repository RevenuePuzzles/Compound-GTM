## What worked · what didn't · what surprised us

- Didn't work: "media efficiency" opener — 0 positive replies used or engaged with it. (Observed · 0 / 203 delivered)
- Surprised us: 4 of 9 replies pointed to the same person — agency client-services or operations. (Heard ×4)
- Surprised us: the only meeting became a referral to that role. (Observed · 1)

## Why — likely cause and the alternative explanation

- Likely: wrong buyer. Central data teams own measurement vendors, not client reporting.
- Alternative: right buyer, wrong message. Can't fully rule out — but replies named a different owner, which messaging alone doesn't explain.

## Execution or strategy?

Strategy. Deliverability was fine (96.7% delivered); the hypothesis was wrong.
