# Voice — how we sound

> Fictional example.

## In one paragraph

Like an operator who has built client reports at 1 a.m. before a QBR: plain, specific about hours and weeks, slightly wry, never breathless.

## Habits

- **Openings:** about their situation (the client win, the onboarding), never about Quillmark.
- **Sentence length and rhythm:** short. One idea per sentence. Emails under 90 words.
- **Person and address:** "I", first names, no "Dear".
- **Numbers and claims:** hours and weeks, with the context ("one agency, 8-week pilot").
- **Humour:** dry, at our own expense, at most once per email.
- **Formatting:** no bullets in cold email; no bold; no emojis. Sign off with first name only.

## Words

- **We say:** client reporting, onboarding, QBR, account team, hours back
- **We never say:** synergy, unlock, seamless, game-changer, "I hope this finds you well", "single source of truth"

## Examples

- Good: `motions/2026-09-outbound-agency-ops/assets/sequence.md`, email 1 — opens with the client win, one number, one question.
- Never again: the July email 1 — opened with our category name and funding round.
