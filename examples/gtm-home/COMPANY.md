# Company — what we actually sell

> Fictional example.

## What we sell

| Offer | For whom | Scope / deliverables | Duration | Price range | Who signs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entry: New Client Launch | One agency, one newly won client | Reporting templates and weekly refresh for one client | 6 weeks | $12,000 fixed fee, billed to the client account | Director of client services |
| Core: Agency Reporting | Whole agency | All client accounts, templates, governance pack | 12 months | Annual, per client account | Agency MD |
| Expansion: Network | Several agencies in one holding company | Shared templates, central governance | 12+ months | Enterprise agreement | Holding-company procurement |

## Sales motion

- **How deals start:** founder-led outbound and referrals; no inbound engine yet.
- **Typical cycle:** entry offer 3–5 weeks; core 2–4 months.
- **Who sells:** founder; one account executive from January.

## Terms

- Entry fee credited in full against a core contract signed within 90 days.

## Capacity and constraints

- Up to 4 new client launches a month with the current team.
- US and UK only; English-language reporting.
