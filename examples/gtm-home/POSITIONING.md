# Positioning — what buyers do instead of us, and why we win

> Fictional example.

## Position

For client-services and operations leaders at holding-company agencies onboarding new clients, Quillmark is the client-reporting platform that gets a new client's reporting live in days instead of weeks, unlike rebuilding decks by hand or waiting in the holding company's central data queue.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | When buyers choose it | Where it breaks | Our counter + proof | How often we meet it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spreadsheets and slides (do it themselves) | Always the default; no procurement needed | When client count grows or a big client is onboarded | Hours saved per week (P-001) | Every deal (Observed · 6 of 6 opps, 2026-09) |
| Holding-company central data team | Agency asks for help building dashboards | Queue of months; built for holdco, not client reporting | "Live before the first QBR" (P-001) | Heard ×4 · 2026-07→09 |
| General BI tool the agency already licenses | Someone on the team knows it | Needs a specialist; every client is a new build | Template library per channel mix — **needs proof** | Believed |

## Pillars

1. **New client reporting in days** — value: first QBR on time · proof: P-001 · answers "we'll just build it ourselves".
2. **Account teams back on strategy** — value: senior hours redirected · proof: P-002 · answers "reporting isn't a priority".
3. **Approved by your holding company's governance** — value: no security fight · proof: **missing** · answers the governance block.

## Objections and answers

| Objection (their words) | Answer | Proof | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| "The holdco already gives us a data platform" | We sit on top of it for client-facing reporting; we don't replace it | Integration note — needs proof | Heard ×3 · 2026-09 |

## Words

- **Use:** client reporting, new client onboarding, QBR, account team, hours
- **Avoid:** "media efficiency", "single source of truth", "cross-channel measurement platform" as an opener
