## Case stories

### A holding-company agency onboarding three new clients

- **Situation:** ~400-person agency won three clients in one quarter.
- **What they tried before:** account managers building weekly decks from platform exports.
- **What changed:** templates per channel mix; automated weekly refresh.
- **Result:** ~30 → ~9 team hours a week on reporting, over 8 weeks (P-001).
- **Quote:** needs approval.
- **Permission:** anonymised.
- **Gaps:** a quote; a second agency for the same result.
