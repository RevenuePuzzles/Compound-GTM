# alternatives — map what buyers do instead of buying from us

The real competition is usually not the vendor on your slide. It is a spreadsheet, an agency, an internal team, a "maybe next year". This move maps every alternative the buyer actually considers and how to win against each.

## Use when

- "Who are our competitors?", "battlecards", "we keep losing to no decision", "why do they pick X?"
- Before `positioning`.

## Reads

`POSITIONING.md`, `winloss` output, `signals` output, competitor websites and pricing pages (via web tools when available), review sites.

## Steps

1. **List the alternatives the buyer sees**, in five groups:
   - Do nothing / live with it
   - Do it themselves (spreadsheets, internal build, existing team)
   - Use an adjacent tool or service they already pay for
   - Hire people (agency, consultant, new headcount)
   - Direct competitors
2. **For each alternative, record:**
   - When buyers choose it and why (in their words, with evidence level).
   - What it genuinely does well — be fair; buyers know.
   - Where it breaks — the specific situation where it stops being good enough.
   - Our counter: the one argument, and the proof, that wins in that situation.
   - Frequency: how often it shows up in deals (`Observed ×n` from CRM or win/loss), or `Believed`.
3. **Verify public facts.** Competitor features, prices and claims change. When web tools are available, check the live page and record the date. Never state a competitor capability as fact from memory.
4. **Rank** by how often each alternative wins against you.
5. **Write battle notes** for the top three: when you meet it, the questions to ask, the trap to avoid, the proof to show. One screen each.

## Output

The alternatives map, top-three battle notes, and the alternative you most underrate.

## Don't

- Don't trash competitors. Buyers discount it and it ages badly in writing.
- Don't treat a feature list as positioning. Win in a situation, not on a checklist.

## Could compound

Alternatives and their frequency into `POSITIONING.md`; verified competitor facts (dated, `Reported`) into `PROOF.md` only if used in assets.
