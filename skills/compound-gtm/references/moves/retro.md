# retro — close out a motion and decide what compounds

The step most teams skip. A short, blameless review of a finished motion that ends with concrete edits to the GTM home.

## Use when

- A campaign, launch, sequence, event or deal has finished (or been stopped).
- "Let's do a retro", "what did we learn?", "close out this campaign".

## Reads

The motion's `BRIEF.md`, `RESULTS.md` (or run `results` first), assets, reply samples, the team's notes.

## Steps

1. **Predicted vs actual.** Put the brief's hypothesis, success and kill criteria next to what happened. One line each.
2. **What worked, what didn't, what surprised us.** Each item states the evidence and its level. Surprises are the most valuable — they show where the home was wrong.
3. **Why.** For each major outcome, the most likely cause and at least one alternative explanation. Keep both if the evidence can't separate them.
4. **Execution vs strategy.** Did the motion fail because of how it was run (list quality, timing, deliverability, follow-up speed) or because the hypothesis was wrong? These lead to different fixes.
5. **Decide what compounds.** Draft the candidate edits, each with destination file and evidence level:
   - play status change and rule updates for `PLAYS.md`;
   - ICP, trigger and disqualifier updates;
   - positioning, objection and proof updates;
   - voice rules from replies and reactions.
6. **Decide what stays local.** One-off context lives in `RETRO.md` only.
7. **Next run.** If the play continues, the one change to make next time.

## Output

`RETRO.md` for the motion folder, and a list of proposed home edits ready for `compound`.

## Principles

- Blameless. The question is what the system should do differently, not who messed up.
- A failed motion with a clear lesson is a good outcome. Record it as carefully as a win.

## Could compound

Everything in step 5 — hand directly to `compound` for confirmation and writing.
