# signals — mine buyer language from calls, replies and reviews

Turn raw conversation material into the most valuable thing a GTM home holds: what buyers actually say, in their own words, counted.

## Use when

- Someone has call transcripts, discovery notes, email replies, support tickets, review-site text, win/loss interviews, community threads.
- Before `positioning`, `sequence` or `asset`, when copy sounds like the company talking to itself.

## Reads

Everything in `field/` or supplied in the conversation. Existing `ICP.md` and `POSITIONING.md` to compare against.

## Steps

1. **Inventory the material.** How many sources, of what type, from which roles and segments, over what period. Note obvious bias (only won deals, only one rep's calls).
2. **Extract, don't summarise.** Pull verbatim or near-verbatim snippets into categories:
   - **Pains** — what is hard, costly, embarrassing, risky.
   - **Triggers** — why they started looking now.
   - **Desired outcomes** — what "good" looks like to them.
   - **Alternatives** — what else they considered, including doing nothing or building in-house.
   - **Objections and fears** — what made them hesitate.
   - **Words** — the specific terms they use for the problem and category (often different from ours).
   - **Proof requests** — what they asked to see before believing us.
3. **Count and attribute.** For each theme: how many separate sources, which roles, which segments. A theme from one talkative buyer is one source.
4. **Compare to the home.** Where does buyer language confirm, contradict or extend `ICP.md` and `POSITIONING.md`? Contradictions are the headline.
5. **Respect privacy.** Strip names and identifying details from quotes unless the source is `public`. Never move one client's verbatims into another client's home.

## Short form

For a handful of notes (under ~10 sources), skip the theme table: list each new snippet with its category, role and count, say which existing home lines it confirms, contradicts or extends, and hand straight to `compound`. Save the raw notes to `field/` when a home exists.

## Output

A theme table (theme · count · roles · two representative snippets), a "words they use vs words we use" list, and the three biggest contradictions with the home.

## Could compound

Buyer-language lines into `ICP.md` (pains, words), `POSITIONING.md` (objections, alternatives), `VOICE.md` (terms to adopt or drop) — all as `Heard ×n`.
