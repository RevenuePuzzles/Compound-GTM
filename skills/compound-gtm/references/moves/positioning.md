# positioning — decide what we say and why we win

Positioning is a decision, not a tagline: for whom, against what, on the strength of what, proven how. This move builds it from evidence and turns it into a message house teams can actually use.

## Use when

- "Rewrite our positioning", "nobody gets what we do", "we sound like everyone else", "messaging framework", "homepage headline".
- After `signals` and `alternatives` have produced fresh material.

## Reads

`ICP.md`, `POSITIONING.md`, `PROOF.md`, `alternatives` and `signals` output, `COMPANY.md`.

## Steps

1. **Name the best-fit buyer and situation** from `ICP.md`. Positioning for everyone is positioning for no one.
2. **Name the alternatives** they weigh (from `alternatives`). Positioning is always relative to something.
3. **List what we have that those alternatives don't** — capabilities, approach, team, data, model. Keep only the ones a buyer has cared about (`Heard`) or that the data shows matter (`Observed`).
4. **Translate each into value** for the buyer, in their words: so what, and how do we know? Attach proof from `PROOF.md`. Value with no proof is marked `Believed`.
5. **Choose the frame** the buyer should file us under — an existing category, a sub-category, or (rarely, and expensively) a new one. Say what each choice costs.
6. **Build the message house:**
   - One-line position: "For [buyer in situation], [us] is the [frame] that [key value], unlike [main alternative]."
   - Three pillars, each = value + proof + the objection it answers.
   - Per buying-committee role: what each role hears first.
   - Words to use and avoid (from buyer language, not brand adjectives).
7. **Test it** with `committee` or `buyer` before shipping. Offer that as the next step.

## Output

The proposed `POSITIONING.md` content, a short rationale tying each choice to evidence, and the riskiest assumption in it.

## Don't

- Don't lead with the company's history, funding or vision; buyers rarely ask.
- Don't ship a position whose core value claim is `Believed`. Say what would prove it first.
- Don't decide for the person. Present the strongest option and one credible alternative frame.

## Could compound

The chosen position and pillars into `POSITIONING.md`; rejected frames and why (so they are not re-litigated every quarter).
