# committee — run the whole buying committee at once

B2B deals are rarely decided by one person. This lens convenes the roles that decide, then synthesises where the piece wins, loses, or gets stuck between them.

## Use when

- Proposals, decks, positioning, pricing pages, deal plans, major launches.
- "How will this land with the whole team?", "who's going to block this?"

## Convene the panel

Build the panel from `ICP.md`'s buying committee. If the home has none, use the default and say so:

| Role | Cares about | Typically asks |
|---|---|---|
| **Champion** | Looking smart, making the case internally, not getting burned | "Can I explain this to my boss in two minutes?" |
| **Economic buyer** | Return, risk, priorities this year | "Why this, why now, why this much?" (see `cfo`) |
| **Daily user** | Their workload, tools, learning curve | "What changes on my Monday?" |
| **Technical / security** | Integration, data, compliance, vendor risk | "What does it touch, and who's accountable?" |
| **Procurement / legal** | Terms, comparables, exit, liability | "What are the alternatives and the terms?" |
| **Skeptic** | Has seen this fail before | "Why will this be different?" |

Each panelist reads independently and returns: what works for them, what worries them, the question they'd raise in the decision meeting, and their vote (support / neutral / block).

## Synthesise

1. **Where they agree** — the strengths to keep and the problems everyone sees.
2. **Where they conflict** — e.g. the user wants depth the economic buyer will never read. Name the trade-off; don't average it away.
3. **The likely blocker** and the specific thing that would move them.
4. **What the champion needs** — the one-page internal case, the answer to the skeptic, the proof they'll forward.
5. **Priority fixes,** in order, each with which panelist it satisfies.

When the Agent tool is available, panelists may run as parallel subagents with only the piece and their role card, so their reads stay independent. Otherwise, run them in sequence and keep their voices separate.

## Output

A panel table (role · vote · main concern · question), the synthesis, and the fix list.

## Could compound
