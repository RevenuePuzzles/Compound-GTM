# onboard — fill the GTM home from what already exists

Turn the company's existing material into a first, honest version of the home — with every line graded by evidence — in under an hour of the person's time.

## Use when

- A home was just created, or a home exists but is mostly empty.
- A consultant is starting with a new client and has a pile of decks, call notes and CRM exports.

## Reads

Whatever the person can give, in this order of value:

1. Call recordings, transcripts, discovery notes, win/loss notes (→ `Heard`)
2. CRM exports: closed-won and closed-lost deals with company, size, source, cycle length, reason (→ `Observed`)
3. Campaign results, reply exports, website analytics (→ `Observed`)
4. Existing sales deck, website, one-pagers, case studies (→ claims to verify, mostly `Believed` until backed)
5. The founder's or sales lead's own account (→ `Believed`, sometimes `Heard` when they relay specific conversations)

## Steps

1. **Harvest before asking.** Read everything supplied. Draft each file from it, tagging every line. Do not ask questions the material already answers.
2. **Interview for the gaps.** Ask at most five questions at a time, highest value first:
   - Name your last three closed-won deals. Why did each buy, and why then?
   - Name your last three losses. What did each do instead?
   - Which customers would you clone? Which would you never sign again, and why?
   - What do buyers say in the first ten minutes of a good call?
   - What do you say about yourselves that no customer has ever repeated back to you?
3. **Separate fact from belief.** Show the person which lines are `Believed`. This is usually the most useful moment of onboarding — the gap between the story the team tells and what buyers actually say.
4. **Draft `VOICE.md` from examples**, not adjectives. Ask for two or three emails or posts they are proud of and one they would never send. Describe observable habits: sentence length, how they open, words they use, words they avoid.
5. **Seed `PLAYS.md`** with whatever they run today, each marked `testing` unless results already meet the bar in `evidence.md`.
6. **Write the files** once the person has seen the draft. Mark gaps as open questions inside the files rather than inventing content.

## Output

- The filled files (or a diff against existing ones).
- A short "confidence map": for each file, how much is `Observed`/`Heard` versus `Believed`.
- The three biggest unknowns, each with the cheapest way to resolve it (five calls, a CRM report, a small test).

## Could compound

Onboarding is the first compound. Afterwards, recommend the move that closes the biggest unknown (usually `signals`, `winloss` or `icp`).
