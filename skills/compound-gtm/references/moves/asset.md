# asset — write pages, one-pagers, posts, decks and ads from the home

Draft any GTM asset that is not a sequence, built from positioning and proof rather than adjectives.

## Use when

- Landing page, service or product page, homepage section, one-pager, sales deck outline, LinkedIn post or article, ad copy, case-study page, webinar abstract, proposal section.

## Reads

The motion's `BRIEF.md`, `POSITIONING.md`, `PROOF.md`, `VOICE.md`, `ICP.md` (which role reads this).

## Steps

1. **Fix reader, moment and job.** Who reads it, at what stage (unaware → problem-aware → evaluating → deciding), and the one thing it must make them do or believe.
2. **Pick the skeleton for the asset type:**
   - **Landing / service page** — situation the reader is in → what changes → how it works → proof → who it's for and not for → offer and next step → objections.
   - **One-pager** — the problem in their words → what we do → three outcomes with proof → how engagement works → next step. Fits one printed page.
   - **Deck outline** — one message per slide; slide titles alone should tell the story. Situation → complication → resolution → proof → offer → ask.
   - **LinkedIn post / article** — one idea, a specific observation or number up front, the reasoning, a takeaway the reader can use without buying anything, a light pointer.
   - **Ad** — one audience, one pain or outcome, one proof, one action. Write three variants that differ in angle, not wording.
3. **Draft from the home.** Use buyer words, pillars and proof entries. Tag each claim with its proof entry in comments.
4. **Mark gaps** as `[NEEDS PROOF]`, `[NEEDS NUMBER]`, `[CONFIRM]` rather than filling them.
5. **Add tracking** for anything with a link: suggest UTM parameters that match the motion slug so `results` can read it.
6. **Run a lens** (`buyer` for pages and posts, `committee` for decks and proposals) and fix before handing over.

## Output

The draft, the claims-to-proof map, gaps to fill, and tracking parameters.

## Could compound

Headlines and angles that perform into `PLAYS.md`; reader questions the asset failed to answer into `POSITIONING.md` objections.
