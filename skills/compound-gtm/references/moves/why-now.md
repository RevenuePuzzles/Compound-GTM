# why-now — find the moments buyers are ready, and the signals that reveal them

Most deals are won on timing. This move maps the events that open a buying window to the public or private signals that reveal them, so outreach lands when the problem is live.

## Use when

- "Why would they buy now?", "build a trigger list", "signal-based outbound", "which accounts should we call this week?"
- A play's reply rate is low and timing is the suspected cause.

## Reads

`ICP.md` (situation layer), won-deal histories (what happened in the 90 days before first contact), `field/` notes.

## Steps

1. **Work backwards from wins.** For each recent won deal, ask: what changed at that company in the three months before they engaged? New leader, funding, merger, lost client, failed vendor, regulation, re-org, budget cycle, public commitment.
2. **List triggers**, each with:
   - **The event** in plain words.
   - **Why it opens a window** — the pain or deadline it creates.
   - **How to detect it** — the signal source (job posts, press releases, filings, leadership changes, tender portals, product changes, review sites, earnings calls, your own website visits) and how reliable it is.
   - **Window length** — how long the moment stays open.
   - **Evidence** — how many won deals showed it (`Observed ×n`) or whether it is a hypothesis.
3. **Rank** triggers by (deals that showed it) × (how detectable) × (how long the window stays open). Hypothetical triggers rank last until tested.
4. **Write the opener angle** for each top trigger: one sentence that connects the event to the buyer's likely pain without pretending to know their internals.
5. **Flag creepy signals.** Some signals are legal but feel invasive when quoted back (personal posts, inferred layoffs). Mark them "use to prioritise, never mention".

## Output

A ranked trigger table, detection notes for each, opener angles, and the one trigger worth testing first with a small play.

## Could compound

Confirmed triggers and their window length into `ICP.md`; signals that proved useless (removed); opener angles that got replies into `PLAYS.md`.
