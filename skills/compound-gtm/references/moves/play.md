# play — design the motion step by step

Turn a brief into a repeatable play: who, when, through which channels, in what order, with which assets, and who does what. Written so someone else could run it next quarter.

## Use when

- After `brief`, or when the person asks "how should we run this?", "design an ABM play", "launch plan", "event follow-up plan", "partner co-marketing plan".

## Reads

The motion's `BRIEF.md`, `PLAYS.md`, `ICP.md`, `POSITIONING.md`, `why-now` triggers.

## Steps

1. **Pick the play shape** that fits the goal and list size:
   - **Signal-led outbound** — small daily lists triggered by events; highest relevance.
   - **Named-account (ABM)** — few accounts, many people per account, coordinated touches across roles.
   - **Launch** — announcement to existing audience and pipeline, then targeted follow-up.
   - **Event** — before (who to meet), during (what to capture), after (follow-up within 48 hours).
   - **Partner** — joint offer or referral motion with a clear "what's in it for them".
   - **Expansion** — existing customers, usage or outcome-triggered.
   - **Deal plan** — one opportunity: stakeholders, risks, mutual plan, next three steps.
2. **Map the steps.** For each: day, channel, who it targets (role), the job of the touch (open, prove, ask), the asset, the owner, and what happens on reply, no reply and negative reply.
3. **Coordinate humans and agents.** Mark which steps an agent drafts or runs and which a person approves or sends. Anything that writes to a CRM, sends email or spends budget needs a named human approver.
4. **Define the instrumentation** — what will be recorded so `results` can read it (source tags, UTM parameters, CRM fields, a simple sheet).
5. **List the assets needed** and route them to `sequence` and `asset`.
6. **Add it to `PLAYS.md`** with status `testing`.

## Output

A step table, the asset list, the instrumentation list, and the `PLAYS.md` entry.

## Don't

- Don't design more touches than the team can personalise. Volume without relevance burns the list and the domain.

## Could compound

The play entry itself. After `results`, its status and rules.
