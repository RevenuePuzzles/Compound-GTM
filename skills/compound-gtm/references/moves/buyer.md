# buyer — read it as the person you're selling to

A cold read by the target buyer, in their situation, on a busy day. Not "is this good copy?" but "would I reply, click, forward or book?"

## Use when

- Any email, sequence, page, post or one-pager before it ships.
- "Would this land?", "read this as a CMO", "does this make sense to a buyer?"

## Set up the reader

Build the reader from `ICP.md`: role, company situation, what they already know, what they care about this quarter, what they're tired of hearing. If the home has no data on this role, say so, and use the draft, the conversation and general knowledge of the role — labelled as an informed guess, not buyer research. Do not invent a personal backstory.

Also ask, out of character, whether this role is the right reader at all, and whether there is a reason to write now. If either is doubtful, say so — it matters more than any line edit.

The reader:

- Has never heard of the company and owes it nothing.
- Reads on a phone between meetings.
- Gets several pitches like this a week.
- Knows their own business far better than the sender does.
- Will not fill gaps generously.

## The read

Go through the piece in order and narrate the reader's reaction at each point where something happens: interest, confusion, skepticism, irritation, recognition, the moment they'd stop.

Then answer, as the reader:

1. **Is this about me?** Where did it feel specific to my situation, and where generic?
2. **Do I believe it?** Which claim made me doubt the rest?
3. **What do I think they want?** Is the ask clear and proportionate?
4. **What would I do?** Reply, ignore, archive, forward to someone, unsubscribe — and why.
5. **What would make me reply?** One change, in my words.

## Output

```
Reader: [role · situation · source of this profile]
Verdict: [what I'd do, in one line]

Moment by moment
> "[quoted line]" — [reaction]
...

The line that lost me: …
The line that almost worked: …
One change that would get a reply: …
```

## Principles

- Stay in character. The buyer does not know the positioning doc or the founder's intentions.
- Quote exact lines. Vague reactions are useless.
- Don't rewrite the piece. Diagnose; `sequence` or `asset` fixes.

## Could compound

Reactions that recur across reviews ("buyers keep tripping on our category word") into `POSITIONING.md` or `VOICE.md` — as `Believed` until real buyers confirm.
