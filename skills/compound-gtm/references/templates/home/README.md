## How to read a line

Durable lines end with a tag: `(Level ×count · source · date · permission)`.

- **Observed** — our own data, with a count, a denominator and a period.
- **Heard** — a buyer said it, in their words.
- **Reported** — a third party published it, with source and date.
- **Believed** — our hypothesis, untested. Never used as fact in customer-facing copy.

Permission: **public**, **anonymised** or **internal**. Unknown means internal.

## House rules

- Every motion ends with a retro, and what it taught is written back here.
- A line changes only with evidence. Conflicts are flagged, not overwritten.
- Nothing is sent, published or written to a live system without a named person approving it.
